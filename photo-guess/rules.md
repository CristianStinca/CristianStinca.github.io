# Photo Guess community rules

_Version 1. Effective: 2026.11.16_

These are the rules every player accepts in the app before their first online game, whether they host it or join it. A game on the same Wi-Fi, or on a TV, doesn't ask for them. The app shows this same text; when it changes, the app asks everyone to accept the new version.

---

## Community rules

Online, you share photos with people who may not be in the room. To keep games fun and safe, everyone agrees to these rules.

### Don't share

- Sexual content, or nudity of anyone without their consent
- Anything that sexualises or harms a child
- Violence or hate
- Photos meant to harass, bully or embarrass someone
- Anything illegal
- Photos you don't have the right to share

### If someone breaks them

- The host lets each player in, and can remove a photo or a player.
- Any player can report a photo or a player, and block a player to hide their pictures on their own phone.
- Reports go to the developer by email. The developer can't see your games, but reads every report and answers it.
- If something is a crime, report it to the police too.

---

## How reports are handled

A report is an email the player sends from their own mail app to cristianstinca.support@gmail.com. The app fills in only the reason, what was reported (a photo or a player), "online game", the app version and the platform; it adds no photo, name, room code, invite or PIN. The player may add a screenshot and a message. Report emails are kept for up to a year after they are answered, then deleted.

The developer reads every report and answers it. Photos and games never reach the developer: they go from the players' phones to the host's phone, end to end encrypted through the relay, which can't read them and stores nothing. So the developer can't see, remove or block anything in someone's game. What the answer can do is explain the tools above, and for anything illegal, point to the police.
